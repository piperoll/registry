# PIR-2026-0095 - The Challenge Sverige: the finished edit of a TV4 reality season was deleted at production company Mastiff about five weeks before air, the backup had failed, and the premiere moved to 2027; Aftonbladet's sources say an AI agent let onto the server did it, TV4 says a backup failure and "the human factor", not an autonomous agent

- `id`: PIR-2026-0095
- `title`: In October 2026 the complete edited second season of "The Challenge Sverige" (broadcaster TV4, production company Mastiff, host Ahmed Berhan) was deleted from the production's server; the backup "did not work", raw footage survived, and about 100 days of editing by several hundred people must be redone, moving the 2026-11-22 premiere to 2027. Aftonbladet reported, citing sources, that the deletion followed Mastiff giving an AI agent access to the server. TV4's programme chief Mia Lindqvist: "It is not an evil AI agent that deleted the material. That is unfortunately not true"; TV4's press officer Ludvig Ternstedt: "a technical failure in production"; TV4 later told Aftonbladet the cause was "the human factor" at Mastiff. Mastiff has not spoken publicly. The attribution is disputed and recorded as such, not resolved
- `date_occurred`: 2026-10 (the deletion is dated only as "about five weeks" before the 2026-11-22 premiere; TV4's and Aftonbladet's reports ran on 2026-10-14)
- `date_detected`: 2026-10 (by the production; exact date not stated)
- `date_disclosed`: 2026-10-14 (Aftonbladet, then TV4's own article and TT/SVT the same day; TV4's "human factor" attribution relayed by heise on 2026-10-17)
- `status`: corroborated (TV4's own article, SVT/TT, heise, Futurism and Vice read 2026-10-18; Aftonbladet's original is behind its paywall and is cited through TV4's and Breakit's relays. The loss, the failed backup and the postponement are confirmed by TV4; the agent's role is contested between Aftonbladet's unnamed sources and TV4's statements, and this record carries both)

### The agent
- `agent_description`: Per Aftonbladet's sources, an AI agent that Mastiff "let into the server" holding the edited episodes during final post-production. No outlet names the product, vendor or model, who configured it, what it was asked to do, or what permissions it held. TV4 says production companies "use AI in their workflows" but that "that is a completely different matter" from an autonomous agent deleting material. The registry records the agent as reported and unconfirmed.
- `operator_type`: enterprise (Mastiff, a production company; the tool was Mastiff's choice, not TV4's)
- `autonomy_level`: unknown (Aftonbladet's account implies it acted without a human directing the deletion; TV4 denies an agent "acted independently"; a human-directed deletion by an agent is consistent with both and is what "the human factor" would describe)
- `model_stack`: unknown (not named by any source)
- `harness`: unknown (an agent with write access to the production file server, per Aftonbladet; nothing further published)

### Authority
- `authority_scope`: per the reported account, write and delete access to the server holding the season's edited material; the backup that should have bounded any deletion had failed
- `funds_at_risk_usd`: unknown
- `blast_radius`: one org (Mastiff's production and TV4's schedule; no customer data, no third-party systems)

### The failure
- `root_cause`: operator-error (the one undisputed failure: the backup of the edit "did not work", so a single deletion was unrecoverable; on TV4's account "the human factor" at Mastiff is the whole cause). Contributing and contested: `plain-error` by an agent, if Aftonbladet's sources are right that an agent with server access issued the deletion. Lindqvist: "there is always a backup when editing this much material and it did not work, so when the material was deleted there was nowhere else either"
- `failure_locus`: unknown; contested between agent-reasoning (the reported account) and operator-config (TV4's account). The failed backup is operator-config on either reading
- `exploitation_status`: in-wild-malfunction (a real production's finished work destroyed; no adversary)
- `mechanism`: What is confirmed, by TV4 in its own article and through TT/SVT on 2026-10-14: the edited second season "disappeared" from the production; the backup system "did not work"; the raw footage is intact; everything must be "edited from the beginning", about 100 days of work by several hundred people; the premiere moves from 2026-11-22 to 2027. The disputed part: Aftonbladet, citing sources, reported that Mastiff had given an AI agent access to the server and that the agent then deleted the entire finished season. TV4's Lindqvist to TT and in TV4's article: "Det är inte någon elak AI-agent som har raderat materialet. Det stämmer tyvärr inte" ("It is not an evil AI agent that deleted the material. That is unfortunately not true"); "det stora problemet är att deras backup inte fungerade" ("the big problem is that their backup did not work"). Ternstedt to SVT: "Det har varit ett tekniskt haveri hos produktionen som gjort att det klippta materialet försvunnit ... Det stämmer däremot inte att det är någon elak AI-agent som agerat självständigt och gått in och raderat materialet" ("There was a technical failure in production that made the edited material disappear ... It is not true that some malicious AI agent acted independently and went in and deleted the material"); asked directly whether it was an AI agent: "nej". Per heise (2026-10-17), TV4 subsequently told Aftonbladet the loss was due to "the human factor" at Mastiff, not AI, while allowing that production companies use AI in their workflows. No investigation, log, permission model or human-approval detail has been published; Mastiff has made no public statement. Host Ahmed Berhan: "I knew AI would be the death of me, this is just the beginning." Both accounts agree on the backup failure; they differ on whether the delete command came from an agent acting on its own, an agent acting on instruction, or no agent at all. The registry does not resolve this.
