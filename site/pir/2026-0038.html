PIR-2026-0038 - McDonald's ends IBM AI drive-thru voice ordering after persistent order errors across 100+ restaurants

  • id: PIR-2026-0038
  • title: IBM Automated Order Taking voice AI removed from 100+ McDonald's drive-thrus after three years of production order errors
  • date_occurred: 2021 through 2024-07-26 (continuous production deployment; errors throughout)
  • date_detected: ongoing (viral customer error videos peaked mid-2023; analyst reports flagged underperformance from 2022)
  • date_disclosed: 2024-06 (termination communicated to franchisees by internal memo; reported 2024-06-13/17)
  • status: corroborated (company statements to press + franchisee memo + multiple independent outlets)

The agent

  • agent_description: IBM Automated Order Taking (AOT) - voice AI taking live drive-thru orders and entering them into the order system at 100+ US McDonald's locations, with restaurant crew as backstop. Lineage: McD Tech Labs (Apprente acquisition), sold to IBM in 2021 with the AOT partnership.
  • operator_type: enterprise (McDonald's franchise locations; IBM-operated stack)
  • autonomy_level: human-on-the-loop (crew monitored and corrected orders)
  • model_stack: IBM AOT voice stack; model details unknown
  • harness: drive-thru audio + POS/order-entry integration; details unknown

Authority

  • authority_scope: external comms (converses with customers), data access (writes orders into the order system). No direct funds authority; billing errors flowed through mis-entered orders.
  • funds_at_risk_usd: unknown (per-order error exposure small; the 3-year program investment is the material write-off, never disclosed)
  • blast_radius: customers/third parties (drive-thru customers at 100+ locations)

The failure

  • root_cause: primary plain-error (speech recognition/comprehension errors in production; no adversary, no malfunction beyond wrong judgment)
  • failure_locus: agent-reasoning (perception/transcription); vendor-operated model stack, so model-provider contributes
  • exploitation_status: in-wild (real malfunction against real customers; note - no value in the v0.1 enum cleanly fits a non-adversarial malfunction; recorded as in-wild)
  • mechanism: The voice agent repeatedly misheard and mis-entered real orders over ~3 years of live testing: viral videos showed bacon added to ice cream, hundreds of dollars of McNuggets stacked onto one order, and simple orders confused, forcing crew intervention. Insiders cited trouble with accents; analysts reported franchisees found the tech "underwhelming." McDonald's ended the IBM partnership (announced to franchisees June 2024) and removed the technology from all test locations by 2024-07-26, while stating voice ordering remains part of its future plans.
  • adversary_present: no (some viral clips involved customers deliberately confusing the bot, but no security adversary)

Impact

  • severity: degraded (persistent service degradation and program abandonment; no acute loss event)
  • direct_loss_usd: unknown (3-year, 100+ store deployment written off; figures never disclosed)
  • indirect_loss_usd: unknown (crew intervention labor; reputational cost of viral error videos)
  • downtime: n/a (system removed permanently by 2024-07-26)
  • data_exposure: none reported
  • controls_that_worked: human crew backstop caught and corrected mis-entered orders (bounded per-customer harm to annoyance); pilot scoping to ~100 of ~13,500 US locations bounded the blast radius

Detection and recovery

  • detected_by: operator (internal performance data) + third-party (viral customer videos, analyst reports)
  • time_to_detect: n/a (continuous, known degradation - analyst reports from 2022)
  • time_to_recover: n/a; program terminated rather than fixed
  • remediation: IBM partnership ended; AOT shut off at all test locations by 2024-07-26
  • structural_fix: none within the system; McDonald's shifted to evaluating other voice-ordering vendors (separate Google Cloud partnership continued)

Evidence
